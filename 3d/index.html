Three dimensions · Carter Bays, 1987

Game of Life in 3D

An empty cell with exactly 5 of its 26 neighbors alive is born. A live cell with 4 or 5 live neighbors survives; every other live cell dies.

Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan

The 3D view needs WebGL, which isn’t available in this browser. The simulation still runs, and the numbers on the right keep updating.

Cells live in a cube and count all 26 neighbors that touch them at a face, an edge or a corner.

B lists the neighbor counts that create a cell and S the counts that keep one alive, out of 26. Write ranges like S4-5 and put commas between numbers above 9. Bays’s four-digit form, like 4555, works too.
